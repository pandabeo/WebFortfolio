About Me

I'm Thinh Bui, a third-year Games student at RMIT University Vietnam. I focus on gameplay prototyping, Unity implementation, and level design.

Most of my work sits between game design and hands-on development: building mechanics, shaping player flow, and polishing mood through level layout, lighting, and iteration.
